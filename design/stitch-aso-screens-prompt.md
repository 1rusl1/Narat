# Narat — актуальное задание на ASO

Редакция 2026-11-09. Это задание, не готовые изображения. [Тексты](aso-store-content.md), [flow](../docs/stitch-ui-prompts.md), [архив прежних изображений](archive/aso-before-2026-11-09/README.md).

Generate separate EN and PL sets of six readable portrait marketing images using the actual approved UI and supplied plant photos. Confirm current App Store export requirements before publication; do not infer them from an old generator.

1. Identify your plant / Rozpoznaj roślinę — photo or catalogue.
2. Understand a possible problem / Sprawdź możliwą przyczynę — preliminary diagnosis with uncertainty.
3. Know what to do next / Dowiedz się, co zrobić — applicable action now and its reason, no treatment timeline.
4. Check before watering / Sprawdź przed podlaniem — observation before explicit logging, no automatic watering.
5. Keep your plant history / Zapisuj historię rośliny — simple photos and care entries in a flat collection.
6. Remember the next check / Pamiętaj o kolejnej kontroli — voluntary reminder for a justified date.

Quiet Garden style: plant photography, warm ivory, forest green, short legible headlines, consistent plant/context. No fabricated statistics, rooms, AI progress comparison, camera light estimate, clarification quiz, monthly report, guaranteed recovery, store products or paid-only basic diary. Do not promise a fixed catalogue size or an unsupported full-care programme.

Before export, verify that each visible action exists in the release build and its Free/paid status is accurate. Preserve legibility in both languages and at thumbnail size. Render and visually inspect every generated image; a prompt or successful generator run is not visual acceptance.
